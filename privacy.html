Privacy Policy

CubeKing 3D • Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

CubeKing 3D ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the CubeKing 3D mobile application for iOS and Android.

2. Camera Access & Face Scanning

CubeKing 3D includes an AI-powered Camera Scanner feature to help you solve physical cubes.

  • On-Device Processing Only: Camera video frames are analyzed entirely on your device in real-time solely to detect the colors of the cube stickers.
  • No Images Stored or Transmitted: We do not capture, record, upload, or transmit any camera images, facial data, or video feeds to external servers or third parties.

3. Information We Collect

CubeKing 3D is designed with privacy by default:

  • No Account Required: You do not need an account, email address, or phone number to play the game.
  • Local Game Progress: Your high scores, solve times, personal bests, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device using encrypted on-device storage.
  • Anonymous Diagnostics: We may collect non-identifiable technical data (device model, OS version, anonymous crash reports) to improve performance and stability.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

In-app purchases (such as upgrading to Pro) are processed securely through Apple's App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or payment information. Transaction receipt validation is handled securely via RevenueCat.

5. Children's Privacy

CubeKing 3D does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app adheres to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Child Safety Guidelines.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
